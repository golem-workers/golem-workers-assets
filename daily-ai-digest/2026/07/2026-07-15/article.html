Agent idea of the day

Turn every inbound lead into an informed human handoff

What this agent does

Read each new sales inquiry, answer common product and policy questions from approved material, ask one useful follow-up, and hand qualified conversations to a person with context intact while holding uncertain replies for review.

Best for: Small sales teams, founder-led businesses, and service companies that receive more inquiries than they can answer quickly.

Give it

  • Approved product documentation, pricing, policy, and customer examples
  • Qualification rules and the questions a human rep needs answered
  • A monitored inbox or form feed plus a CRM destination

Tell it to

  1. Read every new inquiry and identify the customer’s stated need, organization, timing, and unanswered questions.
  2. Draft a reply using only approved source material and cite the internal document behind any policy or compliance claim.
  3. Ask one short follow-up when the request lacks information required for qualification.
  4. Score the lead against the supplied rules and route uncertain or sensitive cases to a person without sending.
  5. Hand qualified conversations to the assigned rep with a concise summary, the full thread, and recommended next action.

Run it: Run when a new sales email or form submission arrives; send a daily exception report for conversations still awaiting human review.

You get

A timely, grounded first response plus a CRM-ready handoff containing intent, qualification evidence, open questions, and the complete conversation history.

Keep a human in control

  • Require approval before sending pricing exceptions, legal commitments, security assurances, or claims not present in the approved library.
  • Never invent product capabilities or hide that an automated assistant prepared the message.
  • Let recipients opt out and route complaints, deletion requests, and high-risk industries directly to a person.

Feasibility: OpenAI reports that its internal inbound sales assistant grounds replies in product and policy material, hands enterprise-qualified threads to reps with context, and improved first-email accuracy from 60% to more than 98% through rep feedback and evaluations. Source: OpenAI →
