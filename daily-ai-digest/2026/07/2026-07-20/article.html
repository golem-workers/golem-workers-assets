Agent idea of the day

Build a trip disruption command center

What this agent does

Watch a multi-leg trip for disruptions, prepare the best recovery options and draft every message, while leaving bookings, cancellations, refunds and payments to a person.

Best for: People managing work, family or group travel with several flight, rail or hotel reservations and tight connections.

Give it

  • Itinerary emails or PDFs for every traveler and leg
  • Calendar access plus traveler preferences, loyalty details and an approved budget range
  • Trusted official airline, rail, airport, hotel and weather status sources

Tell it to

  1. Extract every leg, confirmation number, connection, deadline and contact channel into one timeline.
  2. Check only official operator and airport status sources, and record when each fact was last verified.
  3. When a delay or cancellation appears, compare up to three same-day alternatives with total cost, arrival time and refund conditions.
  4. Draft the operator chat or call script plus concise notices for calendars, hosts and affected travelers.
  5. Ask for approval before any reservation, cancellation, refund request, payment or disclosure of identity details, then open the exact official handoff.

Run it: Start 72 hours before the first leg, check every six hours and again at 24, six and two hours before departure, and run immediately when an operator email or SMS arrives.

You get

One current brief with trip status, the top three recovery options, price and refund conditions, the recommended next action and ready-to-send notices.

Keep a human in control

  • Never book, cancel, check in, request a refund, pay or disclose identity details without per-action approval.
  • Use official operators and airports as the source of truth; label any secondary report clearly.
  • Flag stale or conflicting information instead of filling gaps by inference.
  • Do not interpret visa, compensation or refund law as final advice; escalate uncertain rules to a person.
