Cloudflare gives AI agents wallets with spending limits

Cloudflare Wallets will let people create virtual wallets for agents to buy APIs, MCP tools and digital content through x402 micropayments. Owners will be able to set allowances, merchant allowlists and maximum transaction sizes; account handles are available now, while funding and agent spending are still marked as coming soon.

Hermes Agent adds interruptible voice, sourced research and agent-to-agent links

Hermes Agent 0.20 now supports streaming voice conversations with barge-in and on-device wake words across desktop, command-line and messaging surfaces. It also adds fact-checked citations, signed outbound webhooks and A2A 1.0 links with compatible agents.

Siri’s AI overhaul reaches the iOS 27 public beta

Apple’s rebuilt assistant can hold natural conversations, use personal context to find photos, emails, messages and calendar details, and open or act inside apps for tasks such as directions, music, photo edits and email drafts. It is in the iOS 27 public beta now, with the general release expected in September.

Microsoft maps Orchard across coding, browsing and personal-assistant work

Microsoft’s open Orchard framework trains and evaluates agents in reusable isolated environments. Its recipes cover software repair, browser navigation and personal-assistant work, and the project can train agents inside real harnesses including Codex, OpenClaw and ZeroClaw instead of a simplified substitute.

Source: Microsoft Orchard →

Rubrik limits enterprise agents one tool call at a time

Rubrik Agent Identity checks an agent’s intent, policy and identity before each action, then grants short-lived access only to the requested tool. Enterprises can inventory active agents, reuse Okta or Entra identities, block unauthorized changes and reverse approved actions that still go wrong; pricing and availability were not announced.

Source: SiliconANGLE →

Google removes three ADK workflows after a prompt-injection chain

Google deleted three repository workflows after researchers showed that malicious public issue text could make a triage agent trigger a privileged code-fixing agent. The proof of concept reached runner code execution and exposed credentials, but there is no public evidence of real-world exploitation or a flaw in the distributed ADK Python package.

Agent idea of the day

Run one supervised habit experiment each day

What this agent does

Turn simple meal, activity and recovery records into one realistic daily habit suggestion while keeping health decisions, data sharing and any plan changes under human control.

Best for: People who already collect workout or wearable data and want a low-friction routine for reviewing patterns without adopting another rigid tracking app.

Give it

  • A private folder with meal photos, workout notes and wearable exports you choose to share
  • Your routine, practical constraints and one modest goal
  • Any clinician guidance, injuries, allergies or firm boundaries

Tell it to

  1. Organize only the records I explicitly provide and ask a clarifying question when an image, note or measurement is ambiguous.
  2. Summarize the previous day’s meals, activity and recovery without diagnosing conditions or assigning moral judgments.
  3. Compare the summary with my stated routine and identify the smallest evidence-supported gap I can address today.
  4. Propose one reversible habit experiment with a clear stop condition and an easy fallback for a low-energy day.
  5. Wait for my approval, then log what I chose and review the result before suggesting another change.

Run it: Run once each morning after the latest wearable sync, or manually after a workout; pause when data is incomplete or the user reports pain, illness or a clinician-directed change.

You get

A daily brief with yesterday’s patterns, one approved habit experiment, a fallback option and one question for tomorrow.

Keep a human in control

  • Never diagnose, prescribe, alter medication, set extreme calorie targets or recommend training through pain or illness.
  • Escalate symptoms, injury concerns, eating-disorder signals and conflicts with professional guidance to a clinician.
  • Keep health files in approved private storage and share nothing with another person or service without explicit permission.
  • Treat trends as rough context, state uncertainty and let the user approve or reject every suggestion.

Feasibility: A documented August 2 workflow uses a private folder of Garmin exports, meal photos and workout voice notes with Codex to produce morning activity and recovery briefs, ask clarifying questions, and adjust daily suggestions.
