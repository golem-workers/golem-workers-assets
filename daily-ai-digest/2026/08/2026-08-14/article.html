Agent idea of the day

Build an agent that catches surprise software renewals before they hit

What this agent does

Maintain a verified calendar of software renewals, surface price or terms changes early and prepare a decision packet before any cancellation or negotiation deadline.

Best for: Founders, finance leads and operations teams managing many software subscriptions across email, contracts and vendor portals.

Give it

  • Read-only access to vendor contracts, invoices and renewal email
  • Your approved vendor list, contract owners and current annual spend
  • The notice period, approval threshold and negotiation rules for each subscription
  • A destination for the renewal calendar and review packets

Tell it to

  1. Extract each renewal date, notice deadline, plan, owner and current price, and link every field to its source.
  2. Check vendor email and approved portals for price, packaging or terms changes, and mark anything uncertain for review.
  3. Compare actual use and owner feedback with the upcoming commitment, without inventing savings or business impact.
  4. Prepare a keep, renegotiate or cancel packet with evidence, deadlines and a draft next step for the owner.
  5. Wait for explicit human approval before sending a message, changing a plan, canceling service or accepting new terms.

Run it: Run weekly, switch to daily checks 45 days before a notice deadline, and alert immediately when a verified price or terms change arrives.

You get

A renewal calendar plus a one-page decision packet for each approaching deadline, including source links, current cost, usage evidence, options, the responsible owner and the last safe action date.

Keep a human in control

  • Use read-only access until a person approves a specific external action.
  • Never cancel, renew, purchase, negotiate or send vendor email automatically.
  • Treat contract language, price and usage as unverified unless linked to a source record.
  • Limit access to approved vendors and redact credentials, payment details and unrelated employee data.
  • Keep an audit trail of evidence, alerts, drafts and human decisions.
